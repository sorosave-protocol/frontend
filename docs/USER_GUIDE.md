# Sorosave User Guide

## Getting Started with Savings Groups

### What is Sorosave?

Sorosave is a decentralized savings platform that allows you to create and join savings groups with friends, family, or community members.

### Creating Your First Group

1. **Connect Your Wallet**
   - Click "Connect Wallet" in the top right
   - Select your preferred wallet (Freighter, Albedo, etc.)

2. **Create a Group**
   - Click "Create Group"
   - Enter a name for your group
   - Set contribution amount
   - Add members by their wallet addresses

3. **Start Saving**
   - Each member contributes the agreed amount
   - Track progress in the dashboard
   - Withdraw when goal is reached

### Joining a Group

1. Receive an invitation link from the group creator
2. Click the link and connect your wallet
3. Review group details
4. Click "Join Group" to become a member

### Managing Your Savings

**View Dashboard:**
- Total saved across all groups
- Individual group progress
- Upcoming contribution dates

**Make Contributions:**
- Automatic reminders
- One-click contribution
- Transaction history

### Best Practices

- **Set Realistic Goals**: Start with achievable targets
- **Regular Contributions**: Consistency is key
- **Clear Communication**: Discuss expectations with group members
- **Emergency Fund**: Keep some savings outside the group
